Service work includes

  • Panel upgrades & replacements

    Outdated or overloaded panels upgraded safely to meet today’s demand and code.

  • EV / Tesla charger installs

    Home Level 2 Tesla charger circuits sized at the panel, permitted, and installed to code.

  • Heated driveway install

    Snow-melt systems for Tahoe driveways — mats, sensors, and controls installed for reliable winter access.

    Learn about your heated driveway controls
    • Aerial sensor — A weather sensor (often mounted where it can “see” open sky) that detects precipitation and temperature so the system knows when snow or ice conditions call for heat.
    • Lutron switch — A wall control that lets you turn the heated driveway system on or off from inside the home with a clean, reliable switch.
    • Remote control — Options to control your heated driveway system remotely — start it before you get home without standing at the driveway — focused on the snow-melt system, not turning your whole house into a “smart home.”

  • Lighting, outlets & switches

    New fixtures, recessed lighting, GFCI/AFCI outlets, dimmers, and switch replacements.

  • Hot tub / spa wiring

    Dedicated circuits and disconnects for hot tubs, spas, and outdoor equipment, installed to code.

  • Troubleshooting & repairs

    Breakers tripping, no power, flickering lights — diagnose and fix it right.

  • Permits & inspections

    We handle permits and coordinate inspections so your project stays on track.
